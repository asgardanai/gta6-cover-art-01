# Demo photos

Five AI-generated sample photos, free to use. The app loads them as
built-in demo photos so anyone can try every layout, grade and export size
without bringing a photo of their own. They are only a starting point: use
the upload button or drag and drop to load your own photos instead, and
each panel can hold a different one.

- `pier.jpg` — a smiling older man in a bucket hat and tank top, holding a
  fishing rod on a wooden pier over turquoise water. Shipped with the
  competition kit.
- `boulevard.jpg` — a confident woman in big sunglasses and a linen shirt,
  leaning on the open door of a vintage red convertible on a palm-lined
  beach boulevard at golden hour. Shipped with the competition kit.
- `neon.jpg` — two friends laughing on a neon-lit night street outside a
  diner, pink and teal neon reflected on wet pavement.
- `rooftop.jpg` — a bearded man in an open floral shirt and gold chain,
  standing on a hotel rooftop at dusk with palms and a hazy skyline behind
  him.
- `speedboat.jpg` — a laughing woman in sunglasses on a speedboat at sunset,
  sea spray in the air and palms along the shore behind her.

No photo shows a real person, and none carries text or a logo. Each photo's
subject position is recorded in `app/src/state.js` (SAMPLE_FOCUS) so the
renderer keeps faces in frame by default.
